## 2. Strategy — the rules in force

### 2.1 Indicators (main/, broadcast over MQ)

Candle-bound (CB) model per tf ∈ {15, 60, 240}, per side: a frozen linear model (`indicators/candle_bounds`, exported params in the code) predicts the candle's reach from the frozen feature set (R_/M_/A_/V_/P_ features, `params.FEATURES`; ATR column `1_atr_14_ma_5`). From it, per 1-min row:

- `{tf}_cb_zone_{long|short}` — the entry zone level of the current closed-candle model; `{tf}_cb_inzone_{side}` — the 1-min row touches the zone (the marker the charts draw).
- `{tf}_cbnc_high/low` — the bounds on the **forming** (not closed) candle; `{tf}_cbnc_inzone_*`.
- Filter flags (pass = keep the marker), frozen thresholds: `{tf}_cb_f_trend_{side}` (EMA-25 slope class), `{tf}_cb_f_overshoot_{side}` (deep break of the bound veto, phase 19), `{tf}_cb_f_extdone_{side}` (extreme-done, 2y ladder), `{tf}_cb_f_mcx_{side}`.
- Also on the wire: `{tf}_ema_*`, `1_sar_002_02` (SAR, unused for trading).

Specs: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-cb-markers-main-flow-design.md` (phase 18), `2026-10-17-cb-overshoot-main-flow-design.md` (phase 19), `2026-10-19-cb-zone-run-stats-design.md` (phase 20, the frozen E values).

### 2.2 Open (executor `CbZoneEntryCheck`, spec `executor/specs/2026-10-18-cb-zone-entry-signal-design.md`)

One check per tf (15, 60, 240), any may fire; the decision carries its tf. A long fires when, for that tf:

1. the current 1-min row is in the long zone: `{tf}_cb_inzone_long == 1`, **or** (rule 2) the previous 1-min candle's wick reached `{tf}_cb_zone_long` and it closed back outside the zone;
2. `{tf}_cb_f_overshoot_long` and `{tf}_cb_f_trend_long` pass (extdone **not** used);
3. the executor's own 1-min trade-price MA trend class (Rust port of the visualiser's `trades_ma.js`) is **up** for a long / **down** for a short;
4. `{tf}_cbnc_high` (long) / `{tf}_cbnc_low` (short) is present — it becomes the target; without it the tf does not fire;
5. re-fire control: at most once per (tf, side, zone value) and a 60 s minimum gap per check.

Short mirrored. CB is the only open-signal family armed (SAR/Mock disarmed). An opposite-side fire while a position is open is a **reversal**: the open position is closed (`close_reason = main_close`) and the new one opened in the same second.

### 2.3 Entry price

A limit order at the **book mid rounded onto the tick grid** at fire time (after the DB writes and, on a reversal, the close leg). In paper mode a limit **fills immediately at its limit price** (optimistic, documented in the paper-mode spec; no fees, `is_maker = true`).

### 2.4 Position size (executor `execution::sizing`, book-stop spec §10)

`size = min(RISK_PER_TRADE / |entry − stop|, free_quote × MAX_NOTIONAL_FRACTION / open_price)`, quantised to the lot step. With the paper balance that caps at ≈ 50 000 USDT notional (≈ 4 150–4 200 LINK at ~12 USDT) when the stop is tight, and ≈ 830 LINK when the 1 % placeholder stop is used. Risk actually taken ≤ 100 USDT per trade.

### 2.5 Stop-loss (executor `cb_stop`, spec `executor/specs/2026-10-19-cb-book-stop-design.md`)

At fire time, for the firing (tf, side):

```
E            = CB_STOP_EXPECTED_POINTS[tf][side]      # frozen expected in-zone minutes: 15→2, 60→3, 240→4
streak       = consecutive in-zone minutes already seen for this zone value
minutes_left = max(E − streak, 0) + CB_STOP_BUFFER_MINUTES (1)
rate         = adverse-side volume per covered minute over the last 300 s of trades
               (sells for a long, buys for a short — user decision 2026-10-20, was |buy − sell|)
stop         = the order-book level reached when rate × minutes_left is walked against the
               position (bids downward for a long, asks upward for a short), strictly beyond
               both the last trade and the touch
```

If the walk cannot produce a stop (no trades in the window, zero adverse volume, visible depth exhausted, level not strictly adverse) or the stop is not strictly adverse to the actual `open_price`, the **placeholder 1 % stop** from the entry is used instead and one log line says why. The stop is the executor's own watcher (every trade); paper stop orders never trigger on the venue. No trailing (`TRAILING_FRACTION=0`); the previous trailing-stop logic is off. The streak counter restarts at 0 after an executor restart.

### 2.6 Target and close (executor, spec `executor/specs/2026-10-18-cb-target-trend-exit-design.md`)

- **Target** stored at open: the opposite forming-candle bound of the firing tf — long → `{tf}_cbnc_high`, short → `{tf}_cbnc_low` (`take_profit_price` in `position_log`).
- **Close rule** (`run_advisor_loop`, every trade): while long, once the **max traded price since open has exceeded the target** and the 1-min trade-MA trend class turns **down** → close at the trade price (`close_reason = target`). Short mirrored. Reaching the target alone closes nothing; a trend flip before the extreme reaches the target closes nothing — the position rides to its stop or to a reversal.
- **Reversal**: an opposite-side CB fire closes the open position (`main_close`) and opens the other side.
- **Stop**: `stopped_out` when a trade crosses the stop (§2.5).
- `MIN_RR=0`: no reward/risk gate on the open.
